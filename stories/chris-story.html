New Orleans Football Team Outrun by 69 Year Old Man

When you think of a professional football team, you probably imagine a bunch of young, athletic players, capable of running quickly and stopping just about anyone on the ground. You may imagine that a 23 year old man, making millions of dollars a year with the sole purpose of running a football, would be able to outrun just about anyone. Then it may be a shock to hear that a 69 year old man was able to outrun every running back and several cornerbacks of the New Orleans football team. The senior citizen, who works as a greeter at a local grocery store, said he was "fed up" with the performance of the team, and his wife who was tired of hearing him complain about their running game said "Why don't you go and do it then?" After seeing his performance, the team immediately signed him to a 2 year, 85 million dollar contract, and expects him to be the turning point for the team's performance.
