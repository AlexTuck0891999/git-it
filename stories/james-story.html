Pizza Fired

As It has been brought to our attention, the CEO "Grandma" Peperoni Pizza of the brand new franchise "Grandma's Pizzaria" has been officially spotted and reported to have been selling and buying shares of their company, and influencing others to do the same. As punishment for the insider trading, he has been tossed by company executives into an oven at 425 farenheit for 12 minutes to warm up his frozen heart, and presented to a crowd of hungry children to finish off his deserved fate.
