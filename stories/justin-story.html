Local Man Sets 12 Alarms, Still Somehow Wakes Up Late

A local man says he has finally figured out the perfect way to wake up on time: setting twelve alarms five minutes apart. Unfortunately, he also figured out how to hit snooze twelve times in a row.

Experts say the man may need a thirteenth alarm. He has already announced plans to test the idea tomorrow, assuming he wakes up early enough.

New Phone Update Promises Better Battery Life, Uses Entire Battery Installing

A new smartphone update promises to make batteries last longer than ever before. The only catch is that installing it uses almost all the battery power.

Customers are being advised to plug in their phones, find a comfortable chair, and maybe finish an entire television series while they wait. The company says the update is definitely worth it, but nobody has had enough battery left to check.
