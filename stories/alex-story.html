Local Cat Elected Mayor, Immediatley Fights K-9 Unit

By Thornstrike Nightpaw

Residents of Feather Fall City elected a neighborhood cat named Peaches after his campaign promised longer naps, catnip grown in every garden, and a strict ban on loud noises. He won by a landslide after refusing to participate in any political debates.

The new mayor later approved to dismantle all K-9 units. His office has declined further comment unitl someone opens a can of tuna.
